Before you ask

The short answers.

What do I need to run it?

A Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) on macOS 12 or later, or a 64-bit Windows 10/11 PC. For calls, use headphones so your own call audio doesn't echo back into the mic. The app installs the free audio cable it needs during setup.

How do I actually use it on a call?

Open VoxMorph and pick a voice. In your call app, choose VoxMorph's audio cable as the microphone. That's the whole trick — the person you're calling hears your new voice and installs nothing.

Does my voice go to a server?

No. Everything runs on your own computer. Your audio never leaves your machine.

Can I move my license to another computer?

Yes. Deactivate it on the old computer inside the app, then activate on the new one. Each license covers the number of computers it was issued for.

Is it okay to clone someone's voice?

Only use your own voice, a voice you have permission to use, or a fictional character. Pretending to be a real person without their consent is illegal in many places.

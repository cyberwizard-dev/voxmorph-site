FAQ

Good to know

What do I need to run it?

A Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer) on macOS 12+, or a 64-bit Windows 10/11 PC. For calls you'll want headphones. The app installs the free virtual audio cable it needs during setup.

How do I use it on a call?

Start VoxMorph and pick a voice. In your call app (WhatsApp, Zoom, Teams, Meet…), choose VoxMorph's audio cable as the microphone. That's it — the other person hears your changed voice.

Is my voice sent to a server?

No. All processing happens on your own computer. Your audio never leaves your machine.

Can I use it on more than one computer?

Each license works on the number of computers it was issued for (usually one). You can move it to a new computer by deactivating the old one in the app.

Is cloning someone's voice allowed?

Only use your own voice, a voice you have permission to use, or a fictional character. Impersonating a real person without consent is illegal in many places.
